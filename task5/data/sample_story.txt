Chapter 1: The Orphan
Elara had never known her parents. She grew up in a cold stone orphanage on the edge of the city of Valden.
The matron, a strict woman named Sister Morrow, raised Elara with discipline but little warmth.
By the age of twelve, Elara had learned to be self-sufficient, stealing bread from the market
and teaching herself to read from discarded newspapers.

Chapter 2: The Gift
It was on Elara's sixteenth birthday that strange things began to happen. Books flew off shelves as she passed.
Candles lit themselves when she entered a room. The other children at the orphanage kept their distance.
Sister Morrow called it a curse. Elara called it hers.

Chapter 3: The Merchant
At eighteen, Elara left Valden and traveled west to the port city of Carrath.
She found work with a spice merchant named Aldous, a round-bellied man with a laugh like thunder.
Aldous treated her fairly — she kept his books and in return he let her sleep above the warehouse.
It was the first real kindness she had known.

Chapter 4: The Letter
Three years into her work with Aldous, a sealed letter arrived bearing no return address.
Inside was a single line: "The Academy knows what you are. Come to the northern tower at dawn."
Elara had heard of the Academy — a school of magic hidden in the mountains — but had dismissed it as legend.
Now she was not so sure.

Chapter 5: The Decision
Elara spent three sleepless nights weighing her options. She had no family to consult, no parents to ask for guidance.
In the end, she packed a single bag and told Aldous she was leaving.
He pressed a pouch of gold coins into her hand without a word. She promised to return.
The northern tower waited.
