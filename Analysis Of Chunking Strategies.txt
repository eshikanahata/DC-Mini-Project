Analysis Of Chunking Strategies

Fixed-length chunking
Text is split purely on character count
Pros
- Very fast process
- Simple
Cons
- Context might get broken
- Semantics of the chunk might be lost 

Recursive Character Text Splitter
Hierarchical splitter that tries to split text first by paragraph breaks going all the way down to characters. 
Pros
- Structure of the text like sentences and paras are preserved
Cons
- Slower process
- Not truly semantic but better
